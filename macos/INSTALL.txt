TIMESCREEN 2.0.0
28 styles. 60 palettes. One planet.

INSTALL
1. Drag Timescreen.app to Applications.
2. Eject this disk image.
3. Open Timescreen from Applications or Spotlight.

FIRST OPEN
This open-source build is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized.
macOS may block the first launch. If so, try opening the app, then go to
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
Only proceed if you downloaded the app from the official release and trust it.
Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.

QUICK ACCESS
Keep the app in your Dock, open it with Spotlight, or use its menu-bar clock.
Closing the window keeps the launcher running. Command-Q quits completely.
Settings > Open Timescreen at login is optional and off by default.

DISPLAY
Control-Command-F or the on-screen fullscreen button: native fullscreen.
Command-T: appearance. Command-B: picture controls. Command-K: quiet mode.
Command-[ and Command-]: previous / next style.
Window > Move to Display: choose a monitor.
View > Always on Top / Keep Screen Awake: optional.
Settings > Open in full screen: remember your preferred launch mode.

ONLINE AND OFFLINE
The app automatically opens https://time.paulfleury.com.
If the website is unavailable or older than this app, a complete built-in
copy opens instead. View shows which copy is active.
View > Reload Live Site retries the live website.
Both versions share your native saved setup. No account is required.

UPDATES
Timescreen > Check for Updates opens the official GitHub releases.
Replace the app in Applications after quitting it. Settings are retained.
No silent binary updates, background analytics, or auto-start without consent.

REQUIREMENTS
macOS 13 Ventura or newer. Universal app: Apple silicon and Intel.
